# AIRA Labs Business Operations

## Boundary and ownership

AIRA Labs is the existing division/brand under AIRA Skill City Private Limited. `airalabs.online` serves the public Labs landing page and a realm-scoped staff workspace. The legal employer/company and division remain separate graph nodes. The code does not create a new legal company, workforce model, authentication store, financial ledger, or SIA execution authority.

This is a connected initial business-operations slice. It does not replace the canonical cross-company executive, operations, project, people, and communications modules. See [the validation and activation register](../audit/AIRA_LABS_VERTICAL_SLICE_VALIDATION.md) for tested behavior and launch blockers.

## Connected record flow

1. A public enquiry is accepted only when `SystemConfiguration` key `AIRALABS_INTAKE` contains the strict value `{"enabled":true}` and the current Labs/company hierarchy is active. The pending `OperationalEvent` is an unassigned receipt. Its audit summary contains no contact details; its private metadata holds the submitted contact and consent. Identical submissions use a unique reference. No employee receives arbitrary ownership.
2. A staff member with `labs.sales.manage` explicitly assigns the receipt to an active Labs member with `labs.sales.self` and notification access. The resulting `ExecutiveRecord` kind `LABS_LEAD` carries the lead details, current owner, status, follow-up, qualification evidence, and links. Existing contacts are surfaced for review instead of silently replacing their details. A manager may also create a lead owned by the manager.
3. The allowed lead sequence is `NEW -> CONTACTED -> QUALIFIED -> AUDIT -> QUOTATION_READY -> WON`, with `LOST` where permitted. `QUOTATION_READY` means an approved quote is ready; it does not claim that email or WhatsApp was sent. Client-supplied actor, organization, and arbitrary status changes are ignored or rejected. Version comparisons and command references protect writes against stale and duplicate submissions.
4. `LABS_QUOTE` is a distinct scoped `ExecutiveRecord` linked to one lead. Draft creation and private HTML preview work without commercial approval configuration. Up to 20 items use decimal strings and BigInt minor-unit arithmetic, with half-up item and tax rounding. Available currencies are INR, USD, and EUR; the configured tax basis points are a proposed calculation, not a legal tax determination. The current entry form provides one line item, while the service supports 20. The download is HTML, not a PDF or invoice.
5. Submitting a quote requires an independent `AIRALABS_QUOTE_CONTROL` control point and its canonical human approval chain. An approved request still cannot issue a quote unless `AIRALABS_BILLING` matches the actual company and its approved tax rates, the configured authorizer currently has `labs.quote.issue`, and `CompanyProfile` has matching legal name plus explicit legal verification evidence. These configurations are absent or unverified in the real activation path. A draft's review snapshot is immutable. No automated approval exists.
6. A human records a client acceptance reference against an unexpired issued quote. This is an attestation, not a verified e-signature or external delivery receipt. Only then can an eligible developer and authorized manager create a canonical `Project` linked to the won lead. Canonical project/task permissions remain required; assigning a project owner alone grants no account capability.
7. A delivery manager advances the actual project through planned, work, review, client acceptance, and delivered states. Review and acceptance references are required; open tasks and blockers prevent completion. The generic project editor is blocked from bypassing Labs completion evidence. No invoice, payment, or financial settlement is created.

Lead and quote payloads are discriminated within canonical `ExecutiveRecord` rows. This avoids forcing service prospects into Nice Jobs workforce leads, which have job and incentive rules. `ExecutiveHistory`, `OperationalEvent`, `AuditEvent`, `OperationalRequest`, `Approval`, `Reminder`, `Notification`, `Project`, and `Task` provide the existing control and history mechanisms. No Prisma schema or migration was added for this slice. A later dedicated CRM schema may be warranted if volume and reporting needs exceed these bounded record views.

## Scope, identity, and reminders

The Labs realm requires an existing active `User` and `Person`, active current Labs membership, and existing scoped grants. `labs.sales.read`, `labs.sales.self`, `labs.sales.perform`, `labs.sales.manage`, `labs.quote.prepare`, `labs.quote.issue`, `labs.delivery.manage`, and `labs.delivery.assignee` are registered capabilities; registration grants nobody access. Service methods recheck actual resource scope and owner. Quote visibility follows the linked lead's current owner. Assignment transfers linked quote ownership and revokes the old advisor's self access. Task access also requires canonical project/task permissions.

Labs login uses a dedicated six-digit PIN in the existing hashed credential field, existing session infrastructure, scoped portal access, rate limits, and first-use PIN rotation. `scripts/provision-labs-pin.ts` refuses production and only updates a pre-existing authorized dedicated account in an explicitly confirmed local `.private` SQLite database after validating the operator's current session and permission. It creates no account, membership, grant, or shared Jobs/Boss credential. `director@airalabs.online` is an intended identity, not a provisioned or verified account. Recovery and production provisioning require a separately approved operating procedure. Because an existing Person has a constrained credential/realm relationship, employees needing both Jobs and Labs need an approved identity plan; copying or converting their Jobs account is not automatic.

Follow-up times are stored as UTC instants; browser local date input is normalized to UTC before submission. The dashboard labels UTC reads explicitly. A change cancels prior pending Labs reminders and creates a versioned internal reminder. The manual processor handles at most 25 due Labs follow-ups per call and rechecks current owner, recipient, membership, resource, and time. It does not send external messages or run an endless scheduler. Other reminder namespaces retain their existing processing behavior.

## Activation configuration

- `AIRALABS_INTAKE`: strict `{"enabled":true}` enables public receipt only after the division and parent company are verified active.
- `AIRALABS_QUOTE_CONTROL`: a valid configured independent control point ID; its actual chain, scope, and permission must be reviewed and authorized.
- `AIRALABS_BILLING`: verified `companyId`, `legalName`, `address`, `taxRegistration`, `verificationEvidence`, `authorizedByUserId`, and `allowedTaxBasisPoints`; the canonical CompanyProfile must independently carry `legalInformationVerified: true` and `legalVerificationEvidence`. Presence alone is not proof of verification.
- Local PIN provisioning requires `DATABASE_URL`, `AUTH_SECRET`, `AIRALABS_LOCAL_PROVISION_ENABLED`, `AIRALABS_PROVISION_DATABASE_CONFIRMATION`, `AIRALABS_OPERATOR_SESSION_TOKEN`, `AIRALABS_OPERATOR_SESSION_REALM` (optional, defaults to corporate), and `AIRALABS_INITIAL_PIN`. Values belong only in secure local configuration. The command has not been run for a real account.
- Existing `RESEND_API_KEY` and `NICEJOBS_AUTH_FROM` are for Nice Jobs OTP. They do not constitute a verified Labs commercial sender or consented outbound workflow. A Labs commercial adapter, sender verification, and delivery receipts remain unimplemented.

The overview shows real scoped records and configuration presence, not verified operational readiness. The People list shows eligible current accounts only; it is not an employee appointment register. The UI has bounded first-page views for enquiries, quotes, people, projects, and tasks. The current Finance area is a quote review surface with explicit invoice/payment unavailability, not an invoicing or accounting system. SIA controls remain unavailable and grant no autonomous authority.
