# MAXPASE OS System Constitution

## Canonical Hierarchy

MAXPASE GROUP is the parent business ecosystem. MAXPASE OS is shared operating infrastructure. SIA is the MAXPASE GROUP Virtual CEO and intelligence layer. Company OSs and company operations sit below that shared foundation.

The system must not treat AIRA Skill City as the root. AIRA Skill City is the first company implementation and can influence terminology, but it does not own the architecture.

## Phase 01 Scope

Phase 01 establishes foundations only: application structure, schema, authentication, authorization, organization hierarchy, ownership, audit, configuration, SIA contracts, UI shell, tests, and documentation.

Business modules, full SIA intelligence, and AIRA operational workflows are intentionally deferred.
