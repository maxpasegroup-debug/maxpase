# MAXPASE workplace: Phase 4 manager desk

The Skill City Manager desk is a scoped view over existing `ReportingRelationship`, `Responsibility`, `Task` and CRM `ExecutiveRecord` data. A Director may see it inside the Director workspace; a separately authorized Business Development & Operations Manager may use the Manager view. This phase does not create or assign that proposed role, change anyone's title, or widen an existing role template.

The server shows direct reports only where the viewer has active `reporting.read` and `person.read` grants. Focus history requires `responsibility.read`; tasks require `task.read`; Labs follow-ups require `labs.sales.read`; Startup/Studio follow-ups require `aira.crm.manage`. The UI distinguishes inaccessible data from an empty queue. Phase 8 supersedes the original preview-only summary: scoped count queries now cover underlying direct-report records, while bounded previews and incomplete coverage are labeled separately; see `MAXPASE_WORKPLACE_PHASE_8_OVERSIGHT.md`.

An authorized manager may record a primary focus period of at most 90 calendar days when they also hold `responsibility.read`, `responsibility.manage` and `decision.decide` in the exact scope and have an active reporting relationship to the employee. Dates are interpreted as India calendar dates. The actor enters an approval evidence reference and remains the true audit actor; the referenced source decision must be verified by the organization. Repeating the same command is idempotent; a conflicting replay fails. A changed focus archives the prior focus responsibility but keeps it readable in history. The responsibility is a work-priority record only: it grants no membership, role, project access, product authority, pay or execution permission.

Real access remains blocked until the owner verifies the manager's identity, role, reporting relationships, scoped capabilities and source approval evidence. No production accounts, company records or permissions were changed during development. Local validation uses a disposable SQLite database. Production-domain and physical-device review remain outstanding.
