# Phase 5 commercial controls

The AIRA Startup School commercial register is separate from programme publication and admissions. It stores internal proposed written terms for a canonical Program in an existing ExecutiveRecord, with versioned history and operational audit. Its legal issuer is fixed to the Program's company organization, currently AIRA Skill City Private Limited. A submitted or approved programme does not automatically approve a fee, enrolment, invoice or payment.

Terms preparation requires current programme scope, matching proposed fee/currency and matching written-terms reference. Submission requires a verified CompanyProfile legal name, registered address and tax registration; an independently approved, company-scoped tax policy with current authorization and audit fingerprint; and an effective written-terms document with content hash, independent approval and audit fingerprint. The commercial decision requires a different authorized human than the preparer, a current record version, the unchanged programme snapshot, a reason and an evidence reference. A failed check leaves the record unchanged in its transaction. No tax rate or written terms have been filled in by this implementation.

The tax and terms evidence keys are `STARTUP_COMMERCIAL_TAX:<company organization ID>` and `STARTUP_WRITTEN_TERMS:<company organization ID>:<SHA-256 of terms reference>`. They must be provisioned through a separately reviewed configuration process with immutable source documents and matching AuditEvent records (`startup.commercial_tax_approved`, `startup.written_terms_approved`), including a SHA-256 `valueHash` of the configuration value and its approval evidence reference. **Do not hand-edit a value to simulate approval.** This phase does not provide a configuration writer because issuer, tax and legal documents have not been verified. Until that process exists and real evidence is supplied, submissions fail closed. Existing records are not backfilled or treated as approved.

AIRA Labs quotations continue through the existing `LABS_QUOTE` draft, independent approval, issuer/tax check and issuance path. Its issuer company is checked separately. Nice Jobs incentive, wallet and settlement controls remain in their existing finance service. This work does not create a generic invoice ledger or payment reconciliation engine. Startup School invoice issuance and payment confirmation remain **BLOCKED** pending verified invoice numbering, tax, refund/collection and independent reconciliation policies, with a dedicated tested persistence model or controlled extension. A payment provider response or manual note must not be shown as a paid invoice without a verified, traceable posting.

The Boss view is `/app/boss/brands/[id]/commercial`; authorized Skill City staff use `?view=commercial` in their gateway. All mutations recheck the server-side account and company/division scope. No production database, issuer, payment or provider setting was changed here.
