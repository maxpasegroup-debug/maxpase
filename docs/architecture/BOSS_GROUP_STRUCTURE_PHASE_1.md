## Employment and operating boundaries

`airaskillcity.com` is the intended in-house workplace; `airalabs.online` is the intended customer experience, `airastartupskool.com` the student experience, `nicejobs.online` the part-time workforce experience, and `maxpase.com` the Boss/NEXA centre. Domain assignment is target routing, not proof that DNS, TLS or live account cutover is complete.

The requested Senior Academic Advisor salary is **INR 18,000 per month** and the requested **Junior Developer** salary is **INR 15,000 per month**. These are owner-stated requirements, not active payroll rules, employment contracts, wage payments or verified account appointments. The advisor's BGOS/PBA/GenZ Builder workflow, the director's decision queue and the junior developer's Labs project-only workspace are subsequent implementation phases. Product-specific qualification, targets and reporting belong in configuration of one shared CRM, not three duplicated lead engines. Any payroll, employment decision, published offer, formal quote or payment remains subject to the existing human approval and legal-entity gates.
