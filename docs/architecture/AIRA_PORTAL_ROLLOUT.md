# AIRA portal rollout and cutover

This records the five implementation steps for the AIRA employee and external portal split. It does not attest that a live deployment, email provider, account inventory, or domain session was verified.

| Step | Implemented path | Remaining activation evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Identity and scopes | Boss and authorized directors prepare AIRA Labs employee drafts under the existing company and division. The Full Stack Developer and Business Development Manager templates keep distinct capabilities. Invitations use Skill City. Existing Labs password staff can move after a valid password login, active verified identity and Labs appointment; the move revokes old sessions and audits the change. | Verify real account and role inventory, manager decisions, invitation provider settings and a rollback procedure before staff traffic cutover. |
| 2. Employee workplace | Skill City serves the existing developer desk and a scoped Business Development enquiry queue with audited follow-up changes. Labs no longer renders employee views. | Verify real accounts, record ownership, role-template installation and mobile/browser use. |
| 3. Customer journey | Labs uses its separate customer PIN realm and explicit per-enquiry grant. Grant/provision operators must enter through Skill City and have Labs-scoped permissions; a verified existing dedicated customer account and consent evidence are required. | Customer account registration/invitation and recovery are not implemented here. Do not promise a customer login until the dedicated account, first-use PIN change and grant are verified. |
| 4. Student journey | Startup applicants use a separate Startup account/session. Authorized staff review applications, founder plans and pilot registers from Skill City; learner access still depends on the existing enrollment and evidence gates. | Verified programme authority, tier and terms approval, mentors, participant consent and pilot records are required. |
| 5. Cutover validation | Code keeps portal-specific cookie/audience checks and server-side scope checks. Focused source-level tests cover current routing. | Run isolated PostgreSQL integration tests and live domain, email, revocation, customer, student and rollback checks before production sign-off. |

The revised director role templates include Startup programme review and Labs customer-access management where scoped. They change no live role until the Boss runs the existing reviewed role-template preparation flow. Check existing director appointments before that action. Legacy Labs PIN holders are deliberately excluded from the password-login transition. Identify any staff among them from a verified account inventory and give each a reviewed Skill City credential path; never reinterpret a customer PIN as an employee password. Existing Labs customer grants remain attached to their original user and enquiry records.

No real employee, customer or student account was created in this rollout. No product was published and no programme, payment, salary, or financial permission was activated. The operator should preserve a backup and compare the current records against the migration map before live cutover.
