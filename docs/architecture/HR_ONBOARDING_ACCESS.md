# MAXPASE HR onboarding and access

HR uses one Person, one Employment per legal-company relationship, and historical HrAssignment rows for brands. A Person may have more than one company employment. Creating either record does not activate a User, Membership, or role.

The centralized HR workspace lists registered companies. For an AIRA Skill City company employment, Boss HR can link an existing Person with active employment to the existing staff draft, review its scope and reporting manager, and send the existing single-use email invitation. Delivery requires the configured Resend sender. The employee opens the invitation on the domain prepared for that role, verifies the code and sets a password; the server rechecks active employment, prepared role, manager evidence and portal policy before activating the account. Full Stack Developers and other prepared Labs staff roles use `airalabs.online`; the Administrative Officer uses `airaskillcity.com`. Labs customer PIN accounts remain a separate credential type and cannot enter the staff desk. Resends close earlier codes. HR can view and cancel the latest pending invitation.

Existing active Skill City accounts can receive additional company or mapped brand-division memberships only when the Person has active employment, the brand has an active HrAssignment, the target is under the same legal company, and an existing active human role with the required capabilities is selected. A brand record without a matching active division Organization cannot receive portal access. Suspended access can be resumed only while the employment, role and brand assignment remain valid. Revoked access remains historical; a fresh reviewed appointment is required. Ending an assignment revokes its mapped division access. Ending employment revokes memberships in that company's scope and cancels AIRA invitations without changing another company's memberships.

The employee's Skill City profile displays only their own account, employment, assignments and documents explicitly shared by HR. HR documents default to private. Sharing and removal are audited. Downloads recheck the employee session, active employment, brand assignment and current sharing state.

TEARN and TOP RANK HR records can be prepared and filtered now. Employee portal routing, credential policy, approved role templates, invitation sender and company HR delegation are not verified for those companies. The access page marks the employee portal as **NOT CONFIGURED** and blocks invitation and role provisioning. Their configuration must be approved before activation. No HR record or shared Person identity is evidence of access authority.

The new employee-document visibility migration is additive and defaults to false. Apply the HR migrations only through the controlled release process after backup and migration review; no production migration is performed as part of this development milestone.

## Central HR directory and company setup

The Boss HR directory can create or explicitly link a Person, record employment type and joining date, filter by company, brand, department and employment status, and page through employment records. The employee detail shows account and invitation state, current and historical assignments, and links to documents and access controls. A transfer creates a new assignment and leaves the previous one as TRANSFERRED; the destination department and manager are deliberately blank until reviewed. A manager must have an active assignment in the same brand and a verified active company account. Ending employment closes its assignments and company-scoped memberships while retaining the history. Suspending an AIRA employee account revokes sessions and suspends its company-scoped memberships; resuming the account does not resume those memberships.

HR Settings records proposed portal host, proposed sender and decision reference against each registered legal company. These proposals do **not** activate routing, email delivery, authentication or roles. AIRA Skill City has the existing Skill City portal and prepared-role policy; TEARN and TOP RANK remain NOT CONFIGURED until a verified portal, approved roles, delegated HR authority and sender are implemented and reviewed. The Access & Permissions section links to the existing workforce role editor. Designations do not grant permissions, and newly created roles have no permission by default. Only a prepared and authorized portal role can be granted through the current employee access service.

For the initial AIRA appointments, prepare the reviewed AIRA role templates in HR Access & Permissions, then create or link the employee and activate the company employment. Assign an AIRA Labs developer to the actual AIRA Labs brand and activate that assignment before sending an invitation. The Full Stack Developer role is scoped to the Labs division; its member signs in with the invited password through the Labs employee option. The company Administrative Officer is scoped to AIRA Skill City and signs in through Skill City. Its initial desk provides company coordination and the authorized Startup Skool application review link. Skill Studio admission workflow and wider officer dashboards require separate implementation. Neither title alone grants access, and no invitation can be sent without verified manager and identity evidence. A six-digit code proves invitation ownership; it is not a reusable login PIN.

For a future controlled release, review and apply the additive HR migrations in order: `20261031000000_hr_employment_assignments`, `20261101000000_hr_documents`, `20261101010000_hr_document_employee_visibility`, and `20261101020000_hr_company_configuration`. The runtime readiness manifest includes them. Do not run them against an unidentified database. Required configuration for the verified AIRA invitation route is `DATABASE_URL`, `AUTH_SECRET`, `SKILLCITY_EMAIL_AUTH_ENABLED=true`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `SKILLCITY_AUTH_FROM`, and `SKILLCITY_OTP_SECRET`. Private documents additionally require `CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME`, `CLOUDINARY_API_KEY`, and `CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET`. Configure secrets outside source control and verify the sender domain and delivery before inviting real employees. TEARN and TOP RANK require owner decisions on employee portal destinations, approved role templates, sender identities and HR delegation; recording proposals alone is insufficient.
