# Nice Jobs Programme Pilot and Launch Phase 7

Updated: 2026-10-14 (Asia/Calcutta). Controlled launch remains BLOCKED.

This phase validates the job/programme setup, private content, candidate journey, daily training and manager controls implemented in the preceding six programme phases. It does not replace the older Career Hub experience Phase 6 pilot register, grant real access or activate a provider.

## Scope and Approval Boundary

Only Junior Academic Advisor (JOB-001), Business Automation Manager (JOB-BAM) and Defence Career Mentor (JOB-DCM) are in scope. Internships and First Oppo remain excluded. All three real listings remain unpublished under the [approval register](../audit/NICE_JOBS_PILOT_APPROVAL_REGISTER.md).

Synthetic accounts and prefixed PILOT-JOB fixtures validate shared engine behavior. They are not approved curricula, employer verification or real offers. The rehearsals use the existing isolated fixture division and fixture employer, not a claimed Nidus legal entity or verified real-job division mapping.

For each real job require a named responsible manager, independently authorized reviewers, verified employer/recruitment authority, reviewed engagement/compensation/payment terms, approved performance expectations, complete reviewed Day 1-100 content and policies, and identified consenting existing participants with verified scopes. Filling a register or creating an account is not an authorization.

## Technical Rehearsal

The isolated rehearsal exercises application/disclosure, human screening and offer review, exact orientation/policy acknowledgement, own-offer download, duplicate enrollment protection, frozen curriculum, all 100 practice/evidence reviews and stage milestones, daily reports, pause/resume and an extended final deadline. It then requires final assessment, elapsed approved duration and the separately assigned human readiness reviewer. READY does not activate a worker. Simulated clock progression is not proof that people completed 100 days of training.

Existing regressions cover offer acceptance replay, cross-user/company isolation, private media authorization, content versioning/integrity, report corrections, audit rollback, approval boundaries, controlled reminders, portal isolation and financial/communication restrictions.

The release manifest must contain every actual migration with its exact checksum. OTP and secure-content schema probes complement existing liveness/readiness checks. A training SIA tool selected outside the canonical Nice Jobs scope returns explicit UNAVAILABLE_IN_SCOPE with no records or actions; this never widens authorization.

## Recovery and Monitoring

Back up the database and all referenced immutable private blobs as one reviewed recovery set. Quiesce writers/uploads/processors for the rehearsal. Use the existing read-only SQLite snapshot command, restore to a NEW private path, recover objects, verify integrity/FKs and stored digests, then exercise authorized downloads and denied/revoked access. Database recovery alone is insufficient for uploaded learning content.

The local test restores actual fixture metadata and bytes through the same content service. It also verifies missing-object failure and recovery without modifying the original database. This is not a production restore, encryption/retention audit or measured RPO/RTO.

Monitor liveness and readiness separately. Readiness validates runtime configuration and schema/migration identity, not backups, all object availability, provider readiness, disk capacity or billing. Production operators must supply reviewed alert destinations, ownership, disk/backup freshness thresholds and recorded restore/rollback evidence.

## Performance and Operating Costs

Instrumented local service samples record elapsed time, SQL count/duration and response size for twenty authorized training-detail reads per synthetic role after a 100-day rehearsal. These single-process, low-concurrency samples are not HTTP/device latency, representative production p95/p99, large-media load or capacity certification.

Current uploads are bounded to 64 MiB and two uploads in flight per process. Downloads load the complete object and verify its digest before constructing the response, including range responses. Production must measure peak memory, disk and bandwidth under approved concurrent media load before launch; local tiny-image recovery tests do not certify video capacity.

Do not invent provider prices or a monthly budget. The owner/operations reviewer must record verified billing rates and limits for compute, persistent database/private-object volumes, backup copies and retention, download egress, OTP email, monitoring and separately approved integrations. Calculate storage from all retained versions and referenced blobs, not only the current curriculum. WhatsApp and payment usage remain disabled unless separately approved.

## Manual Completion Gate

Record actual Android/iOS and desktop browser versions, small/large text viewports, keyboard-only journeys and supported screen-reader results. Check focus order, labelled controls/errors, confirmations, file/media alternatives and captions/transcripts, date-picker timezone interpretation, long labels and return/company context.

Managers must identify the next action within 30 seconds and finish review/follow-up without losing scope. Initial representative staging request targets remain p95 <=2 seconds and p99 <=5 seconds; dataset and concurrency, hosting budget and backup RPO/RTO still require agreement. Synthetic service timings cannot sign off these targets.

For every check record target/release/schema, synthetic or approved account references, exact scenario, result, evidence reference, reviewer and unresolved defect. Stop on authorization/isolation/replay/rollback failure, wrong employer/policy/curriculum version, misleading progress/earnings, missing consent or unapproved employment/money action. Resolve critical defects and rerun affected and broader regressions before any real launch decision.
